Watch a star eclipse its partner, colour by colour.

NASA's TESS timed these eclipsing binaries. SPHEREx photographed them thousands of times, each time at a different infrared wavelength. Sort SPHEREx's pictures by where the stars are in their orbit, not by date, and the eclipse appears.
